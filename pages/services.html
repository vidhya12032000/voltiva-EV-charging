QUICK ANSWERS

Questions about charging?

What charging speeds are supported? +

Voltiva stations can support different AC and DC charging configurations. The available power depends on the station and vehicle compatibility.

Can I find available chargers? +

Yes. Station availability can be checked through the charging network interface before starting a session.

Does Voltiva support fleet operations? +

Fleet tools can be used to monitor vehicles, charging activity, energy consumption and operational information.

Can businesses monitor charging performance? +

Business operators can use centralized dashboards to understand station usage, charging activity and network performance.
