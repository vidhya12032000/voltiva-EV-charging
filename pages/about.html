OUR STORY

From a simple idea to a smarter charging network.

Voltiva started with one question: Why should charging feel complicated? We set out to connect EV drivers, charging infrastructure and intelligent technology into one seamless ecosystem.

2022

The idea begins.

We imagined a charging experience where drivers could find, understand and use charging infrastructure without friction.

2023

Growing the network.

Charging points expanded across high-demand locations, making reliable charging more accessible to everyday EV drivers.

2024

Intelligence enters charging.

Real-time station information, load monitoring and smarter charging experiences became part of the Voltiva ecosystem.

2026

Building intelligent mobility.

Voltiva is evolving beyond charging into a connected mobility platform for drivers, fleets and businesses.
